The problem

It isn't your shampoo. It's your water.

Most U.S. homes run hard water: calcium and magnesium picked up from limestone on the way to your tap. Those minerals bind with soap instead of rinsing clean and leave a film on everything they touch.

So you buy the clarifying shampoo, the gloss treatment, the glass cleaner, the descaler. You do everything right and still step out feeling like something's off. Soft water shouldn't require owning a house.

Hair

Mineral film coats the cuticle. Your color fades faster, ends feel straw-like, and the conditioner you paid for can't reach the strand.

Skin

Soap curd stays behind after rinsing. That tight, dry feeling after every shower is often residue, not your skin.

Glass

White spots on the door within days of cleaning it. You squeegee, they come back.

Fixtures

Scale clogs nozzles and narrows the spray. Your pressure drops a little more every year.

Why Osso

We know the feeling. We engineered the fix.

We built Osso for people who've been told soft water means a plumber, a garage and a few thousand dollars. You shouldn't have to own the building to have a better shower.

Osso runs the same ion-exchange chemistry as a whole-home system. Water passes over beads of cation-exchange resin that hold calcium and magnesium tightly and release a trace of sodium in their place. Instead of a tank and a bag of salt, the resin lives in a sealed pod sized for one shower and one month.

Questions

Questions you might have.

Will it fit my shower?

Osso fits any shower arm with a standard ½″ NPT thread, which covers nearly every U.S. shower. Rain heads on ceiling arms work too. Handheld-only setups need our hose adapter, sold separately.

Does it lower water pressure?

The ring is rated for 2.5 gallons per minute, the federal maximum for showerheads, so it won't be the bottleneck. Many people notice stronger spray after a few weeks as scale stops building up in the head.

Does softened water add sodium?

Ion exchange adds roughly 8 mg of sodium per liter for each grain of hardness removed. That's a small amount and it rinses off. Osso water is for showering, not drinking.

How do I know when to change the pod?

The indicator band on the ring shifts from white to blue as the resin saturates. You can also see the resin level drop through the clear pod. Subscribers get a reminder a few days before their next delivery.

Can I change my subscription?

Yes. Change pod count, change cadence, skip a delivery, pause, or cancel from your account. No fees and no minimum term.

What happens to used pods?

Every delivery includes a prepaid return mailer. Spent pods are cleaned, the resin is regenerated, and the capsules are reused or recycled.
